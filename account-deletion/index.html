Account & Data Deletion

Morning Pages is local-first. You can remove the app’s profile and reading data directly from the device where it is stored.

Before you delete

Export any reading archive you want to keep. Local deletion cannot be undone.

1. Delete from the app

  1. Open Morning Pages.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Review both confirmation messages, then choose Delete permanently.

The app first releases Morning Gate restrictions and cancels its alarm, then removes its local data.

2. Data removed from this device

  • Profile details and profile images.
  • Book library records, imported book files, cover images, and current progress.
  • Reading sessions, elapsed time, page history, reflections, streaks, statistics, tasks, and verification records.
  • Reading goals, wake schedule, reminders, theme, language, Morning Gate selections, and other settings.
  • The cached subscription entitlement on that device. An active purchase can still be found later with Restore Purchases.

3. What is not removed

Deleting app data does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.

  • Subscriptions and transaction history held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.
  • Backup, CSV, JSON, or Goodreads files you previously exported to another location.
  • Emails or support messages you sent to us.

Delete exported files from the destination where you saved them. Manage or cancel a subscription through the store account used to purchase it.

4. If you cannot open the app

Uninstalling Morning Pages removes its local app container according to your operating system’s behavior. For help with a deletion or privacy request, email support and include the platform you used.

Request deletion helpVisit support

5. Manage a subscription separately

On iPhone or iPad, open Apple Settings, select your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. The exact labels may vary by operating-system version.
