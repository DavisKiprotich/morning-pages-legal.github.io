Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Morning Pages processes, why it is needed, where it is kept, and the choices available to you.

Effective date

September 30, 2026

Morning Pages is designed around local storage and user-controlled permissions.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Morning Pages mobile app and this website. For privacy questions or requests, email devmile45@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the app

Morning Pages may store the information you provide and create while using the app, including:

  • Your profile name and optional profile image.
  • Reading goals, wake time, scheduled reading days, reminder choices, theme, language, and other preferences.
  • Your book library, imported file references, physical-book photos, titles, authors, page counts, current pages, and completion status.
  • Reading sessions, elapsed reading time, pages completed, streaks, reflections, verification records, tasks, and statistics.
  • Morning Gate configuration, permission status, and the apps or categories you choose to restrict.
  • Subscription entitlement and product status supplied by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.

You do not need to provide complete payment-card details to Morning Pages. Store providers process payment information.

3. Local storage, exports, and backups

Your profile, library, reading progress, sessions, verification data, and preferences are primarily stored on your device. Imported files and saved images are kept in app-controlled local storage.

When you choose to export a reading archive or create a backup, Morning Pages prepares a file and opens the device share sheet. You choose where that copy goes. Any copy saved to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, or another service is then handled by that destination under its own terms and privacy policy.

Automatic cross-device cloud sync is not currently provided. Restore works from a backup file you select.

4. Permissions you control

  • Camera: used when you photograph a profile image, physical-book cover, or page for verification.
  • Photo library: used only when you choose an existing profile or book image.
  • Documents and files: used when you choose an eBook to import or a backup to restore.
  • Notifications and alarms: used for reminders and the wake schedule you configure.
  • iOS Screen Time: Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity permissions are used to shield the applications, categories, or websites you select during an active Morning Gate.
  • Android app access: Usage Access, Accessibility, display-over-other-apps, and foreground-service capabilities are used to detect and cover selected applications while the Morning Gate is active.

You can change permissions in your device settings. Removing a required permission may stop the related feature from working.

5. Morning Gate selections

On iOS, Apple provides opaque selection tokens for the applications, categories, and web domains you choose. Morning Pages uses those selections to apply and remove Screen Time shields. On Android, the app stores the package identifiers of applications you select for blocking. These selections are used to enforce your configured gate and show the correct blocked-app message.

Morning Pages does not use these permissions to read the contents of other applications, messages, calls, or web pages.

6. Book imports and verification

For physical books, you may provide front and back cover images and page photos. The app may process a page photo with on-device text recognition to identify a page number. For digital books, Morning Pages processes the imported file on your device to display supported content and track progress. You are responsible for importing only files you are permitted to use.

7. Purchases and service providers

Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat may process subscription transactions and send Morning Pages information such as an anonymous app-user identifier, product identifier, purchase status, renewal status, and entitlement status. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

The app includes a pluggable analytics interface, but its default configuration does not send analytics events to an external analytics provider. Morning Pages does not sell reading data and does not use third-party advertising.

8. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or replace it through a restore. The in-app deletion flow releases the phone blocker, cancels the Morning Pages alarm, and removes the app’s local profile, books, progress, sessions, streaks, settings, verification photos, imported files, and other app-owned local data.

Deleting local data does not cancel a store subscription and does not remove exported copies saved elsewhere. See Account & Data Deletion for steps and limits.

9. Security

We use platform storage, app sandboxes, operating-system permission controls, and store-managed purchasing to reduce unnecessary access. No security method is perfect. Keep your device passcode secure and use trusted destinations when exporting backups.

10. Children

Morning Pages is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so we can review and remove it where applicable.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
