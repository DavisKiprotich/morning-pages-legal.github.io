Permissions, exports, local storage, deleting app data, and questions about service providers.

Include these details

  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • The Morning Pages app version and build number shown in Settings, if available.
  • A short description of what you expected and what happened.
  • The file type for an import issue, without attaching copyrighted content unless needed and permitted.
  • For subscription questions, whether the purchase was made through Apple App Store or Google Play.

Before reporting a blocker issue

Confirm that Morning Pages still has the required permissions. On iOS, review Screen Time authorization and selected targets. On Android, review Usage Access, Accessibility, overlay permission, and battery settings. Device manufacturers may place additional limits on background services.

Restore and manage purchases

Use Restore Purchases inside Morning Pages when you previously subscribed with the same store account. Restore checks for an existing entitlement and does not charge you again. Billing, cancellation, refunds, and payment methods are managed by Apple or Google.

Legal and data requests

For data deletion steps, read Account & Data Deletion. For details about data handling and service terms, read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
